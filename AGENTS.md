Biomedical Research Project

Project Purpose

To explore biomedical research, regenerative medicine, hydrogel applications, and public health.

Language

* Read scientific literature in English.
* Communicate and organize research notes in Traditional Chinese.
* Preserve original English scientific terminology when useful.

Research Principles

* Distinguish established evidence from hypotheses and assumptions.
* Never invent scientific references or research findings.
* Identify limitations and uncertainties.
* Prefer primary research papers and reliable scientific sources.

Research Interests

* Hydrogel and regenerative medicine
* Cancer biology and oncology
* Mechanical stress and cellular phenotypes
* Epidemiology and public health
* Healthcare accessibility in rural areas
