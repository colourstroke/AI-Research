About this project

AI-Research is a learning and research project connecting scientific literature, seminar discussions, public health data, and interdisciplinary perspectives.

The purpose is to develop research questions through critical reading, evidence evaluation, and synthesis.

Research interests

Environmental Health

Pollution, chemical exposure, toxicology, and environmental epidemiology.

Gender & Public Health

Gender-related differences, health inequalities, and access to healthcare.

Taiwan Public Health

Connecting local health data, environmental conditions, and community needs.
